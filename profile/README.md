### 🚀 About Nexus
**Nexus** is the flagship **Research, Space, and Technology club** at **Manipal University Jaipur**. We are a passionate collective of engineers, researchers, designers, and innovators pioneering projects at the intersection of deep tech, aerospace research, robotics, and astronomical exploration.

### 💻 Nexus Web Development
The **Web Development Wing** powers the digital frontier for Nexus. We design and engineer immersive, modern, high-performance web applications, interactive 3D visualizations, research publication portals, and telemetry dashboards.

- 🪐 **Immersive Web Experiences**: Building cosmic interactive interfaces with Three.js, WebGL, and modern animation systems.
- 🔬 **Research & Project Showcases**: Architecting platforms to publish scientific papers, space mission telemetry, and club innovations.
- ⚡ **Cutting-Edge Stack**: TypeScript, Next.js, React, Node.js, Tailwind CSS, and cloud technologies.
- 🤝 **Open Collaboration**: Fostering an inclusive developer ecosystem for students at MUJ and beyond.
